Meeting minutes show the actual meeting start and end, using only the end time
when both fall on the same day, each point's proposer and a compact header that
omits empty fields and repeated time zones, localize point, agreement and
follow-up statuses, list related points and decision details, show a point's
destination when it moves to another meeting or series, and show its follow-up
actions in the point and in the final recap with their origin point, kind, Odoo
activity type, responsible, due date and status. Empty description and minutes
sections are omitted.
