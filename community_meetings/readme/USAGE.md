Create a meeting series to define recurring meeting defaults, or create a
standalone meeting. Use the guided meeting and agenda wizards to select the
date, attendees and agenda points. When preparing a meeting in a series, its
active recurring-point templates are selected by default in the agenda step.
Deselect a point to skip it for this meeting only; the series template remains
available for later meetings. Every selected template creates a separate point
occurrence, linked to the previous occurrence so its notes and result can be
reviewed without sharing the point itself. During the live meeting, record discussion
notes, agreements, dissents and follow-up work on each point. Prepare the
minutes and submit them for approval when the meeting is complete.

After approval, open the read-only approved minutes from **Approved Minutes**
in the meeting form or live meeting. In the meeting list, use the **Approved**
filter and the **View approved minutes** row action. The action opens the latest
approved revision; use **Back** to return to the form, list or live meeting.

Access is controlled by the Proposer, Reader, Editor, Approver and Manager
groups. See the [user guide](https://oyale.github.io/odoo-meetings-minutes/) for
role details and workflows.
