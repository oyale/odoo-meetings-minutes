Administrators can configure meeting roles, agenda types, meeting kinds,
dissent reasons and follow-up kinds from the Community Meetings menus.
Series can define default attendees, estimated duration, agenda behavior and
follow-up policies. To set standing agenda items, open the series and use its
Recurring agenda tab. Add and order templates with their point type, priority,
estimated time, responsible person and description. Deactivate a template to
stop proposing it in future meetings; existing agenda points are unchanged.
See the user guide's administration section for details.
