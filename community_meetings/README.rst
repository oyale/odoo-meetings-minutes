Community Meetings provides a structured workflow for preparing, running
and approving operational meetings. Meeting series provide reusable
defaults; agenda points retain their identity across meetings, while
each participation records discussion notes, decisions, follow-ups and
minutes for that session.

Approved minutes are stored as immutable revisions with a generated PDF.

Configuration
=============

Administrators can configure meeting roles, agenda types, meeting kinds,
dissent reasons and follow-up kinds from the Community Meetings menus.
Series can define default attendees, estimated duration, agenda behavior
and follow-up policies. See the user guide's administration section for
details.

Usage
=====

Create a meeting series to define recurring meeting defaults, or create
a standalone meeting. Use the guided meeting and agenda wizards to
select the date, attendees and agenda points. During the live meeting,
record discussion notes, agreements, dissents and follow-up work on each
point. Prepare the minutes and submit them for approval when the meeting
is complete.

Access is controlled by the Proposer, Reader, Editor, Approver and
Manager groups. See the `user
guide <https://oyale.github.io/odoo-meetings-minutes/>`__ for role
details and workflows.
